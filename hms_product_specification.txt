# Hospital Management System (HMS) Specifications

## Global Design Rules
- **Color Palette:** Dark Navy (#0B1929) background, White panels, Electric Teal (#00C9B1) accent, Soft Amber (#F5A623) alerts.
- **Typography:** DM Sans (UI/Body), Playfair Display (Headings).
- **Layout:** Card-based with glass morphism effects (backdrop-blur, semi-transparent borders).
- **Navigation:** Dark navy sidebar with Lucide icons. Top navbar with hospital logo, user role, and logout.
- **Components:** Striped tables, centered modals with backdrop blur, floating label inputs with teal focus.

## Modules to Build
1. **Auth & Access Control:** Login page (role selector), Admin panel (user management, role badges), Login logs.
2. **Patient Management:** Registration form (auto-PID), Patient list (search/filter), Detail cards (admission status).
3. **Doctor Management:** Directory grid (specialization badges), Add/Edit modal, specialization filters.
4. **Appointment & Scheduling:** Weekly calendar, Booking panel, Appointment status chips (Confirmed/Rescheduled).
5. **EHR:** Profile tabs (History, Lab, Prescriptions), Timeline view, Doctor notes, File uploads.
6. **Billing & Payment:** Itemized bills, Payment selector (Cash/Card/Online), Receipt modal, Insurance claim tracker.
7. **Pharmacy & Inventory:** Medicine inventory table, Stock level bars (Green/Amber/Red), Low stock alerts.
8. **Laboratory Management:** Test request list, Result entry form, Status chips, Linking to EHR.
9. **Room & Bed Management:** Visual floor plan grid, Color coding (Occupied/Available/Cleaning), Bed allocation modal.
10. **Reporting & Dashboard:** KPI cards, Patient admission line chart, Revenue breakdown bar chart, Export buttons.
